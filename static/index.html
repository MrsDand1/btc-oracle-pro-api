Glossary — words used on this page

Target
The line price has to beat at the window close. It is the 60-second average price in the minute before the window opened.
Sigma (σ)
The market's usual 1-minute wiggle — how much it normally moves in one minute. Moves are judged against it: 2× sigma is a big move for that market, 3× sigma is bigger still.
Cone
The shaded band on the window chart: the target plus and minus one sigma (±1σ), projected to the close. A rough range for normal wiggle, not a guarantee.
Held call
A call whose direction stayed the same for 3 straight minutes in this window. Without that, the page says no held call and the market is choppy.
ENTER / WAIT
ENTER appears only when confidence is 60 or higher and the call has held steady. WAIT means no call right now.
Book imbalance
Which side of the Kalshi order book — YES or NO — has more contracts stacked on it right now. Shown for display only; it does not feed the signal.
YES price
What the market charges for the YES side, in cents. It is the market's own price for "price finishes above target" — for example, 62¢ reads like the market pricing that outcome around 62%. It is the market's price, not the Pulse signal.
